How to format your CSV

Each row is one question. Only domain, description, correct and choice0 are required.

ColumnMeaning
domainTop-level category (used for filtering and the score breakdown)
subdomainOptional finer category, shown as a badge
difficultybeginner / easy / medium / hard / expert
descriptionThe question text
explanationShown in feedback after answering
hintOptional hint or formula reminder; a 'Show hint' button appears
calculatorSet to 'true' to show a calculator on the question (+ - * / ^ ( ), log, ln, e, pi)
correctIndex(es) of the correct choice(s), e.g. 0 or 013 for multi-answer
pointPoints for a fully-correct answer (default 10)
choice0..9Answer choices choice0 ... choice9 (at least two)

A correct value with more than one digit (like 013) becomes a "select all that apply" question.

Wrap math in dollar signs to typeset it, e.g. $\frac{1}{2}$ or $x^2$.

Example
domain,subdomain,difficulty,description,explanation,hint,correct,point,choice0,choice1,choice2,choice3
math,algebra,medium,Solve $2x=10$.,Divide by 2.,Undo the multiplication.,1,15,3,5,8,10
